About St. John Leonardi Model Schools

Discover our educational community, values and commitment to excellence.

Who We Are

St. John Leonardi Model Schools is an educational community located in Amakohia Layout, Owerri, Imo State, Nigeria.

The school seeks to provide students with an environment where academic development, discipline, moral formation and personal growth can work together.

Our Educational Vision

We believe education should develop the whole person. Students are encouraged to pursue knowledge while developing responsibility, discipline, character and respect for others.

Our School Community

The school community includes students, teachers, administrators, families and other members who contribute to the educational experience of the students.

Our Principal

The principal provides leadership and guidance to the school community, supporting the school's academic, moral and administrative activities.

Our Values

Our school places emphasis on academic excellence, discipline, responsibility, moral development, service and respect.
